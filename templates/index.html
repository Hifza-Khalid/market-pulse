    Built for everyday investors.

    Market Pulse turns noisy market data into clear, honest insight — so you can make decisions with confidence, not confusion.

    What we do

    A daily briefing, written in plain language.

    Every morning we quietly gather the latest prices for every stock on your list, study how each one has been moving, and produce a short, readable summary that lands in your inbox before you've had your first coffee.

    No financial jargon. No overwhelming charts. Just the numbers that matter, presented in a way that anyone can understand.

    How we think

    Honest forecasts, always.

    We don't claim to know what tomorrow will bring — nobody does. Instead, we show you a sensible range of outcomes based on months of past behavior, so you can see both what might happen and how uncertain the prediction really is.

    When there isn't enough information to make a confident call, we say so. We'd rather tell you "I'm not sure" than invent a number that sounds convincing but isn't.

    What you can do

    Tools that meet you where you are.

    Set a price alert and we'll email you the moment your target is reached. Run the investment calculator to see what an old purchase would be worth today. Compare stocks side by side on a single chart. Read today's headlines for each stock, automatically sorted into positive, neutral, and negative.

    Everything is designed around one principle: respect for your time. If a feature doesn't help you make a better decision, it doesn't belong here.

    Who it's for

    Anyone curious about the market.

    Whether you're following your first stock or comparing years of investments across exchanges, Market Pulse adapts to your level. It's built for people in Pakistan and around the world who want to understand the market without becoming full-time traders.
